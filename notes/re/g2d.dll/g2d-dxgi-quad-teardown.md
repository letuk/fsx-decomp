the local deleting wrapper at reference count zero. The original uses
virtual dispatch there. The inline constant uses the destructor alone.
The standalone deleting wrapper has a compiled source entry and its
final table binding remains open. The initialized-holder reset at
2067e9c0 was subsequently connected in the
[window state reset package](g2d-dxgi-window-state-reset.md).
