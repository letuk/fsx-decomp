The source connects both routines in src/g2d-pilot/dxgi_shader_loading.h.

api.dll ordinal 106/108 allocation pair and scope-bound release for the
two temporary D3D interfaces. The profile table, final D3DX bindings
and resource insertion are explicit dependencies. The descriptor field
interpretations follow Microsoft's [shader description](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/d3d10shader/ns-d3d10shader-d3d10_shader_desc)
and [input binding description](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/d3d10shader/ns-d3d10shader-d3d10_shader_input_bind_desc).
